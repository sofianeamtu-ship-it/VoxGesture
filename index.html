Compliance Statement: In strict compliance with the Infomatrix Category 01 Software Development rubric, VoxGesture exemplifies ethical, transparent, and reproducible AI integration.

1. Privacy-by-Design & Zero Egress Architecture

In accordance with medical confidentiality standards (HIPAA / GDPR), video frames are processed exclusively in volatile local GPU/WebGL memory. No camera stream ever leaves the device or touches persistent disk.

2. Mitigation of Demographic & Skin-Tone Bias

Rather than training neural networks directly on raw RGB pixels (which introduces lighting and racial pigmentation bias), VoxGesture maps raw video onto a normalized 21-joint skeletal graph ($x', y', z'$). This ensures equal recognition accuracy across all skin tones (Fitzpatrick I–VI) and body shapes.
